Sign In

An active @andrew.cmu.edu account is required to sign in.

  • A page that's only for signed-in people gets the box. Say what signing in lets you do, not just that you must.
  • A link that needs sign-in keeps its place in the text with " [login required]" after it.
  • The header's Sign In link, beside the menu button on every page, is in the kit: see Sign In and Sign Out.
  • Always "Sign In" and "Sign Out", never "Log In".

Button Box

One wide button at the top of the sidebar, for the one thing to do or take away from the page. Two ways, both from CMU pages:

  • In a colored box, with a ghost button, as on University Communications & Marketing's Guidelines for News Images, which offers the guidelines as a PDF.
  • On its own, a solid button in a simple box (no background), as on Computing Services' video production page, where each section's sidebar has a Request button for that service.

Say what the button does and, for a file, what kind it is.

On This Page

Links to the sections of a long page go in a sidebar link list, as on First-Year Orientation's schedule page. It's the template's list left-rule box: each link points to a section heading's id, and the template scrolls to it. Put it first in the sidebar, and leave it off pages short enough to see at a glance.
