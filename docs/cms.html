Home Page for an App's Info Site

An app's Cascade site explains the app and sends people to it. Its home page works best in this order, as on cmu.edu/syllabusregistry:

  1. Hero: a tagline, one sentence, a button into the app, and screenshots.
  2. Path cards: one per audience (students, instructors, administrators), each leading to its own page.
  3. How it works: three or four short steps, with a callout under them for the one thing people most need to know.
  4. Question teasers: the most common questions, linking to the FAQ.

Colors

Use these custom properties in your own stylesheet instead of copying the values, so every site moves together if they change. Each comes from one of three places:

  • Brand: CMU's official colors, from brand.cmu.edu, such as Carnegie Red and the Tartan palette's threads.
  • Template: the colors cmu.edu's template draws on the web. Some are brand colors exactly; others are a shade darker so text on them stays readable, such as its green, close to Green Thread. The template's own color classes are on CMU Standard.
  • Kit: pale tints for backgrounds and dark shades for small text on them, such as the green of a "Syllabus Available" pill. They aren't brand colors: no brand color is pale enough for a background behind text, or dark enough for small text on a tint, in every hue. Use them only for those jobs, and a brand or template color for anything large, such as a band or a bar.
